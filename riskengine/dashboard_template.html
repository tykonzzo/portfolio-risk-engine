Portfolio construction and risk-return analysis

Portfolio Risk Engine

A SQL pipeline turns raw NSE price files for 15 stocks into returns, volatility and correlations. Three long-only portfolios are then backtested walk-forward and compared on Sharpe ratio and maximum drawdown.
